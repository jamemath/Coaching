Paste Availability / Duty

Copy straight from the sheet: first line is the dates, then one line per name with their 1/0 values for each date — works for coach or player rows, it matches names against your existing rosters automatically. Rows of pairings like "Dave & James" are picked up as the Duty rota automatically, whatever the row's labelled. A row of kick-off times like "11 A" / "10:45 A" / "TBC" is picked up too and sets time + home/away. A row labelled "game" (e.g. "Seeding game", "Cup QF", "No game") fills in a placeholder opponent on new fixtures. Dates don't need to be in order — Jan/Feb/Mar/Apr/May/Jun dates are read as the season's second half automatically, so cup dates pasted ahead of the league run still land in the right year.
